The brain after surgery

This is one patient's brain on the scan taken after surgery. Orange is the treatment target: what is left of the enhancing tumour, plus the cavity where the tumour was removed.

The guideline margin

Blue is the 15 mm margin that the 2023 ESTRO-EANO guideline recommends treating, trimmed at the skull, the falx and the ventricles.

Each model now commits to the voxels it ranks highest, treating exactly the volume of the guideline margin: distance on one side, the lead model on the other. The recurrence is still hidden.

The truth test

{{n_models}} models were compared on 63 LUMIERE patients. The rule picked one challenger, its weights were frozen and fingerprinted, and the plan was posted on OSF. Only then were 40 new RHUH-GBM patients downloaded, and the script ran once. It refuses to run a second time.

Does more complexity help?

Every model against distance, from the simplest to the most complex. The line at zero is distance itself.

What it means

The answer is evidence about where glioblastoma recurs in public data. It is not a treatment prediction, and margin recommendations remain the guideline's. See Learn for the limits.
